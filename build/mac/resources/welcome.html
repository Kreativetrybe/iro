@NAME@ for DaVinci Resolve Studio

Your sound effect library, sorted into categories inside Resolve. Search, preview, find sounds in the key of your music, and drag them onto the timeline.

  • Needs DaVinci Resolve Studio 18 or later (workflow integration panels are a Studio feature).
  • Close DaVinci Resolve before installing, or restart it afterwards.
  • Your sounds stay where they are. Nothing is copied or uploaded.
  • Free to use, for personal and paid work.
